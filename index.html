The process

From sign-up to settlement

Here's exactly what happens after you hit submit — no mystery, no runaround.

  1. 01

    Submit your information 2 min

    Fill out the express onboarding form below with your authority details, equipment, home base, and lane preferences. Upload your carrier packet documents at the same time.

  2. 02

    Verification & welcome call Same day

    We verify your authority is active and in good standing with FMCSA, confirm your insurance limits and certificate holder details, and walk through your rate floor, preferred lanes, home time, and any places you won't run.

  3. 03

    Broker setup 24 hrs

    We submit your packet to brokers in your operating area and get you set up in their systems. Your dispatcher builds a target lane list based on where you live and where the freight pays.

  4. 04

    First load offer 24–48 hrs

    You get a rate confirmation with the rate, miles, deadhead, commodity, appointment times, and broker credit score. Nothing gets booked without your yes. That's what "no forced dispatch" actually means.

  5. 05

    On the road Ongoing

    We handle check calls, appointment changes, and problems at the dock. You keep driving. Before you're empty, your next load is already lined up.

  6. 06

    Paperwork & getting paid At delivery

    Send us your signed BOL and receipts from your phone. We assemble the invoice packet and submit it to your factor or the broker, then follow up until it's paid.

              Questions

              Straight answers

              How fast can I actually start getting loads?

              If your authority is active, your insurance is current, and you submit your packet through the express form, we can typically have you set up with brokers and sending you rate confirmations within 24 hours — often the same business day. The most common delay is an insurance certificate that needs to be updated by your agent, so check that date before you upload.

              Do I have to take every load you send me?

              No. We do not do forced dispatch. Every load comes to you as an offer with the rate, miles, deadhead, commodity, appointment windows, and the broker's credit score. You say yes or no. We only book after you approve.

              What exactly does your fee cover?

              Load sourcing and negotiation, broker setup and carrier packets, rate confirmation handling, trip planning, check calls, invoice packet assembly and submission, and pursuit of detention, layover, TONU, and lumper reimbursements. There is no setup fee and no monthly minimum. You're not charged for loads you source yourself, and you're not charged on a load that cancels before pickup.

              Can you dispatch me if my authority is brand new?

              Yes, though it's an honest uphill climb. Many brokers require 90 days to 6 months of active authority before they'll set you up, and some load boards weight new MCs lower. We work the brokers who do take new authority, focus you on lanes where that matters less, and build your history deliberately so more doors open at the 90- and 180-day marks.

              Do I need my own authority, or can I lease on?

              Dispatch service requires your own MC operating authority — we work for you, not the other way around. If you're currently leased on to a carrier, you can still talk to us about the transition. We'll tell you what it costs and how long it takes before you commit to anything.

              Do I have to use a factoring company?

              Not at all. If you factor, send us your Notice of Assignment and we'll submit invoices straight to your factor. If you bill brokers directly, we'll submit to the broker's AP department and track the aging. Either way the paperwork is on us.

              What insurance do brokers expect?

              The common baseline is $1,000,000 auto liability and $100,000 cargo, with some shippers requiring higher cargo limits for electronics, pharma, or high-value freight. We'll be listed as a certificate holder so we can forward your COI to brokers without bothering your agent each time.

              Is there a contract, and how do I cancel?

              The dispatch agreement is month to month. Cancel any time with 30 days' written notice — no penalty, no early termination fee. We'll finish dispatching loads already booked and close out your invoicing so nothing gets stranded.

              Who's responsible if a broker doesn't pay?

              A dispatch service isn't a guarantor of broker payment, which is exactly why we check credit and days-to-pay before booking. If an invoice ages past terms we chase AP, escalate to the broker's bond or trust, and help you file with the surety. We'd rather turn down a load than put your money at risk.

              What time zones and hours do you cover?

              Load planning happens during business hours across all U.S. time zones, and a dispatcher is reachable 24/7 for anything happening on the road — a detained truck, a rejected load, a breakdown, a rescheduled appointment. You'll have a direct number, not a queue.
